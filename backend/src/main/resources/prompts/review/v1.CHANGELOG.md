# review/v1

2026-09-22. The first version, written from Document 4, Prompt 2: Review.

**Text**: the prompt of the document, placeholder for placeholder: the policy in its own data section, the draft as
compact JSON with one field or rule per line and each rule's id first, and the six numbered instructions. The system
prompt is the shared conduct alone; the reviewer never sees the author's few-shot example.

**Two settings changed from the document on the first live run, Document 4 first**:

- Timeout 180 s and output cap 16,000 tokens, not 45 s and 4,000: the strong model timed out at 45 s three times on
  the lending draft. The review of one draft took 40 to 80 seconds.
- A coverage entry that is not a list of rule ids is dropped rather than failing the review: one answer in 19 wrote
  the coverage map as `{"1": {"ruleIds": [...]}}`, and its thirteen findings were sound.

**Measured** (2026-09-22): one live run over the demo draft (the canonical author/v1 recording) and the draft of each
of the 18 labeled policies (the expected rule set with its seeded mutations), on `gpt-5.6-terra`, recorded in
`fixtures/eval/recordings/openai/review/v1/`:

- The demo draft: the conflict of paragraphs 1 and 8 and the ambiguity of paragraph 4, SF-2 and SF-1 of the lending
  policy, which step 1 shows.
- Reviewer recall 79 of 90 seeded defects (0.88; target 0.80). Missed: 7 ambiguities, 3 conflicts, 1 unsupported;
  every gap, duplicate and injection was found. Precision is the evaluation runner's (day 11).
