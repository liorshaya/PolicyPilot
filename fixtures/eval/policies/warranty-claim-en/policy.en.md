Appliances are covered for 36 months from the delivery date shown on the invoice.

Claims filed after the coverage period are rejected.

Damage caused by improper installation, power surges without a certified surge protector, or heavy or commercial use voids the coverage.

Repairs with an estimated cost above 1,500 ILS are referred to a service supervisor.

An appliance that fails a third time within the coverage period is replaced; replacements require supervisor approval.

Coverage for refurbished appliances is 12 months.

Claims that meet the conditions above are approved for repair. Anything not decided is referred to the service desk.
