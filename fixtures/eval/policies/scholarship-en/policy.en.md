Undergraduate students with a grade point average of at least 88 in the previous academic year are eligible for an excellence scholarship of 6,000 ILS.

Students must have completed at least 30 credit points in the previous year.

Students whose household income per person is below 3,500 ILS per month receive an additional need supplement of 3,000 ILS.

Students with a serious disciplinary record in the last two years are not eligible.

Students in their final year who have an average of 85 to 87.9 are referred to the scholarship committee.

Graduate students are not covered by this policy and are rejected.

Students with an average of at least 85 are eligible for the excellence scholarship.

Applications that meet the conditions above are approved. Applications not decided by the rules above are referred to the scholarship committee.
