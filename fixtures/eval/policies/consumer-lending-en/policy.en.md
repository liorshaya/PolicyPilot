A personal loan is granted to an individual aged 21 to 65 at the time of application.

The loan amount is between 5,000 and 120,000 ILS and the repayment term is between 6 and 72 months.

The applicant must have been employed for at least 6 months with the current employer, or self-employed for at least 18 months. Applicants aged 18 and above who have been employed for at least 24 months are also eligible. Unemployed applicants are not eligible.

Net monthly income must be at least 7,000 ILS. For self-employed applicants the average of the last 12 months is used.

The monthly installment is computed with an annuity schedule at an annual interest rate of 10.8%.

The debt-to-income ratio, defined as existing monthly repayments plus the new installment divided by net monthly income, must not exceed 45%. Applications with a ratio between 40% and 45% are referred to an underwriter.

An applicant with two or more adverse credit events in the last 24 months is rejected. An applicant with exactly one event must provide a guarantor with a reasonable repayment history; without a guarantor the application is referred.

Applications that meet every condition above are approved. Anything not decided by the rules above is referred for manual review.
