A full-time student at a recognized institution is entitled to a 25% discount on municipal property tax for the apartment in which the student lives, for up to 6 years from the start of studies.

A reservist who served at least 20 reserve days in the previous calendar year is entitled to a 5% discount; 40 days or more entitle the reservist to a 10% discount.

Discounts under this policy are not combined: the higher discount applies.

The discount applies to an apartment of up to 120 square meters. A larger apartment receives the discount on the first 120 square meters only.

An applicant with outstanding municipal debt of more than 2,000 ILS is referred to the collection department before any discount is granted.

Applications submitted after 30 June 2026 are referred to the discounts committee.

A student who is also a reservist receives both discounts.

Applicants who meet none of the conditions above are rejected. Applications that meet a condition are approved at the applicable rate, and anything not decided is referred to the discounts committee.
