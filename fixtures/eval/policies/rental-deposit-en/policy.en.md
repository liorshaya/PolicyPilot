The security deposit is refunded within 21 days after the tenant vacates, subject to the conditions below.

A tenant who gives less than 30 days of written notice forfeits half of the deposit.

Repair costs beyond normal wear and tear are deducted against invoices. If the claimed damage exceeds the deposit, the case is referred to legal review.

Unpaid rent or utilities are deducted. If the unpaid amount exceeds the deposit, no refund is made.

Pets not declared in the lease incur a cleaning fee of 500 ILS.

Late notice results in a deduction of one month of rent.

Refunds that meet the conditions above are approved. Anything not decided by the rules above is referred to the property manager.
