# The Register's checklist, walked

The design's checklist is section 12 of [the Register spec](../design/register.html): every pull request that touches
the interface is reviewed against it, and its "don't" column is a hard rejection. Document 9's phase 6 walks it once
through the whole product, as Document 7's day 16 walks the Brief's Definition of Done. Walked on 2026-09-29 on the
branch `register/phase-6`, in Chromium at 1376×900 in both themes and at 390×844, every API call answered from the
committed fixtures, then on the cloud site after the merge, on the screens that call no model, in both themes. A line
a browser can see is checked on every screen by
[`frontend/e2e/register.spec.ts`](../../frontend/e2e/register.spec.ts): the seven screens in both themes, the palette
open, and the four demo steps on a desktop and on a phone. Each of those checks is shown to find a fault planted for it
by [`frontend/e2e/checklist.spec.ts`](../../frontend/e2e/checklist.spec.ts). A line that failed was fixed in phase 6,
each fix with its test red first, or is listed under the README's known limitations.

## Don't

| # | The line | Result | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | No Inter, Roboto, Heebo, Arial, system-ui; no purple, indigo or violet; no gradients anywhere; no gradient text | Met | The checklist on every screen: the faces loaded are IBM Plex and Frank Ruhl Libre, each the first asked for wherever text is drawn; no purple hue; no gradient in a background, a border image or a mask |
| 2 | No explanatory subline under a title; no motto in the chrome; no counts in the navigation that are not something to act on | Met | Under each title stands its provenance line alone; the principle is written once, on the gate, as the spec composes it; the rail's one count is the Rules screen's findings to acknowledge (`AppShell.test.tsx`) |
| 3 | No cards inside cards, no rounded-xl, no drop shadows on panels, no glassmorphism, no blurred blobs, no dotted backgrounds, no tinted alert boxes with an icon on the left | Met | The checklist: no radius over 4px but the paragraph chip's pill and a mark's circle, a shadow only under what floats, no backdrop filter and no blur, no gradient (which a dotted background is); a note is a bar and the speaker's mark, with no fill (`Note.test.tsx`) |
| 4 | No dark sidebar; no icon-with-label nav; no icon in a tinted rounded square; no emoji; no sparkles glyph; no switches | Met | The rail is paper in the light theme and its screens are words (`AppShell.test.tsx`); the checklist finds no emoji, no sparkle and no switch |
| 5 | No row of four KPI cards; no pie or donut; no chart with two axes; no zero figure taking a quarter of a row | Met | Three figures in a band over the outcome bar, the evaluation errors a line under them (`Dashboard.test.tsx`); the charts are bars on one axis |
| 6 | No tracked-out uppercase labels or status tags; no "→" welded to links; no exclamation marks; no "Get started", "Submit", "Learn more"; no "cache" or "tokens" in a credit officer's chrome | Met as the spec writes it | The checklist: capitals only in the seal, no arrow welded to a link, no exclamation mark, none of the three phrases; `register.lint.test.ts` refuses letter-spacing outside the seal, the band rows and the figures. | 7 | No chat bubbles, no avatars, no typing animation; no spinner in the middle of a screen; no shimmer; no dashed empty-state frame | Met as the spec draws it | A turn of the thread is a mark and a column, no bubble; the checklist finds no picture and nothing moving at rest; loading is still rows and a line, empty the sheet's ruled lines (`States.test.tsx`). | 8 | No centred numbers, no proportional figures in a column, no colour as the only carrier of a status, no red pill on something that is not a decision | Met | The checklist's figure checks; every tag and every mark carries its word (`StatusTag.test.tsx`, `Severity.test.tsx`); "Blocks publishing" is an ink outline |
| 9 | No zebra stripes, no vertical dividers, no outer table border, no hidden columns without a scrollbar and a count | Met on a desktop; on a phone, a known limitation | `Table.css` as the spec writes it; the decision table's strip counts the fields to the right over a scrollbar in view. | 11 | No fade-in on every section, no hover scale, no bounce; no modal for a detail view; no toast for something the screen should say in place; no lorem ipsum or placeholder people | Met | The checklist finds nothing moving at rest and no placeholder text; a detail opens in the margin, and a dialog serves only the irreversible (`Overlay.test.tsx`); a toast confirms and offers the next step |

## What the walk changed

Found by the checks' first run, before anything was built, or by the walk, and fixed in phase 6 with the owner's
answers where the spec disagreed with itself: the unchanged candidates under 4.5:1; the phone's six screens clipping
Audit log; the Hebrew rule set name in three provenance lines; the unified diff clipped on a phone; section 02's deep
link, which no screen drew; the assistant's cited case, which opened the Cases screen but not the case; and two screens
with two primaries. The cloud walk found one line more, a set of values standing past its cell on the published rule
table, fixed in #173. One line stays open on a phone and is listed under the README's known limitations.
